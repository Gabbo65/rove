Search & address bar

Search engine
Used for searches from the address bar and the New Tab page
Show search suggestions while typing
What you type in the address bar is sent to your search engine to get suggestions. Your history, favorites and open tabs are always matched on this computer only.

Tabs

Sleeping tabs
Tabs you haven’t looked at for a while are unloaded to free memory. They reload, with their back/forward history, when you click them. Tabs playing sound never sleep.
Put tabs to sleep after
Pinned tabs come back every time you start the browser. Right-click a tab to pin it. Search your tabs with ⇧⌘A.

Phone remote

Control Rove from your phone
Scan the QR code (⇧⌘P) on the same Wi-Fi. Each new phone has to be allowed here on the computer once. Off = Rove doesn’t listen on the network at all.
Show the QR code when no phone is connected
The QR code card appears in the corner of each window until a phone connects, and comes back when it disconnects. You can always open it with the phone button in the toolbar or ⇧⌘P.
